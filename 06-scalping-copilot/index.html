Your broker

Where to find these on your phone

MetaTrader 5 → Quotes → tap the symbol → open its Specification / details view. Read off Contract size, Digits, Tick value, Volume min, Volume step and Stops level.

These are the numbers everything else is computed from, and they are set by your broker rather than by the market — the same instrument can have a different contract size on a different account type at the same firm. If you cannot find the screen, your broker's contract-specifications page on the web carries the same figures.

Once they are in, this tool does not need to know whether the account is a spread bet or a CFD. The ticket takes lots either way.

Your trading window

Everything in the tool is ranked against this window: which hours are worth trading, which releases land while you are at the screen, and which happen after you have stopped.

Decision support, not advice. It reads bars; it cannot see order flow, your broker's actual fill, or the contents of a release. Nothing here is backtested.
